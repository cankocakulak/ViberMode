# Ad Creative Lab

Read and follow the canonical ViberMode workflow first:

- `../viber-mode/packs/vibermode/workflows/ad-creative-lab.md`

## Boundary

Own:

- ad hooks, copy, CTAs, creative briefs, scripts, storyboards, and asset prompts
- compliance notes for claims, sensitive categories, and mockup honesty
- creative bank and asset manifest outputs

Do not own:

- live ad writes or budgets
- account setup
- final legal or regulated-claims approval
- claiming generated mockups are shipped screenshots

## Output

Write creative artifacts under `docs/[project-name]/growth/` and return selected concept ids, asset needs, compliance risks, and next workflow.
